Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

This policy explains how the mobile game Color Army ("the game", "we") handles information.

1. Information we collect

We do not create accounts and we do not collect personal information such as your name, email address or location. Your game progress (levels, gold, stars, upgrades and settings) is stored only on your device.

2. Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address and basic device information to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), Google's consent form asks for your permission when you first open the game, and you can change your choice later. Learn more in how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads; iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).

3. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

4. Deleting your data

You can delete all game progress at any time in the game: Settings → "Reset progress". Uninstalling the game also removes it from your device.

5. Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows when it was last changed.
